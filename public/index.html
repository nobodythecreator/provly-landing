Questions providers ask.

Do I need a card to start?

No. Create your organization, use every module for 30 days, then choose a plan from inside the app when you're ready.

Can I bring my data over from Therap?

Yes. Export clients and staff from Therap as CSV and the migration module maps and imports them, so you're not re-typing records to switch.

Does EVV really replace my vendor?

Clock in and out is built into the service note for EVV-required codes, with original times preserved and any edit reason-coded. There is no separate EVV subscription.

Where does my data live, and who can see it?

In Postgres, behind row-level security: the database itself scopes every query to your organization, so nobody outside it can read your records. Access rules live in the database, not just in the app.

What happens when my census grows past my plan?

Nothing stops. You'll see a nudge that you've outgrown the plan and a one-click path to move up. Caps are guidance, not walls.

How do I cancel?

From your billing portal, any time. Your data stays yours.
