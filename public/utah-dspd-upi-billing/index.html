How it works

Units come from approved notesOnly approved service notes count. For EVV-required codes, a unit needs both an approved note and an EVV visit, and Provly bills the lesser of the two.
Quarter hours are added up for youMinutes are totaled per person, per code, per day, and rounded to the nearest quarter hour.
Absences are respectedDays a person is away are recorded with a reason, and aren't billed.
MTP needs a trip recordTransportation bills only on days with a complete pickup and drop-off record.
UPI's file stays UPI'sProvly fills the file you downloaded from UPI. It keeps every line, name, PID and rate exactly as UPI wrote them, and writes only units and dates.
You review before you uploadAnything that needs a second look is flagged first. When you mark the upload done, those notes lock as billed.

Questions providers ask.

What is the e520 payment file?

The monthly file you download from UPI, fill with the units you're billing, and upload back.

Who uploads the file to UPI?

You do. Provly prepares it, and you review and upload it.

How are EVV codes handled?

A unit counts only when an approved note and an EVV visit both support it.

Can I see what Provly filled before uploading?

Yes. Every line is visible, and anything unusual is flagged for review.
