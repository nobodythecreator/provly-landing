Personal Budget Assistance (PBA) for Utah DSPD providers

Managing someone's money is where a provider's paperwork gets the closest look. Provly keeps each person's PBA ledger, reconciles it against their bank statement every month, and walks you through the forms the DSPD scope of work asks for, from the reconciliation to the Support Coordinator's Form G.

How it works

The month in four steps, with deadlinesReconciliation (Form B) by the 5th, review with the person (Form C) by the 10th, administrative review (Form D) by the 15th, and the SC report (Form G) by the 30th. Each step shows when it's due soon or overdue.
Statements in, not retypedImport the bank statement file, or let Provly read a PDF statement right in your browser, then review it before anything is saved.
Only the right people see itA person's PBA record is visible only to their assigned PBA Manager, reviewer and quarterly auditor, plus your compliance director and owner.
Every change has a reasonEntries can be corrected, with a reason, until the month is signed. Signing Form B seals it. Reopening a sealed month takes two-factor sign-in and a written reason, and the re-sent Form G is labeled “Amended”.
Signed in ProvlyStaff attest, and the person or guardian signs on screen, with a recorded exception when they can't.
Asset warnings before they're a problemProvly checks countable assets at every reconciliation and warns you along the way. SSI or Social Security back pay can be marked so it isn't counted.
Form G built for youProvly builds the Support Coordinator report as a PDF with every signature block filled in, and records when and how it was sent.

Questions providers ask.

Can I show a Support Coordinator a sample Form G?

Yes. Provly can produce a sample built from made-up data, which is handy before a referral.

What if a receipt is lost?

Record a lost receipt affidavit (Form F) against the entry.

What if we find a mistake after the month is sealed?

The owner or compliance director can reopen the latest sealed month with a written reason. The original stays on record, and the corrected Form G goes out marked “Amended”.
